# Text Integrity

- Never use Unicode U+2013 or U+2014 in authored repository text.
- Use commas, colons, parentheses, semicolons, or separate sentences instead.
- Do not echo internal constraints, prompts, editorial rules, or optimization strategy into user facing copy.
- Do not mention AI authorship, AI detection, humanization, prompt instructions, SEO manipulation, or GEO manipulation in published copy.
- Do not write meta commentary about how text was produced.
- Preserve established capitalization for game terms, places, factions, characters, products, and systems.
- Keep punctuation natural and consistent within each locale.
