# Internal Linking

- Link entities when the destination adds useful context, not every time a term appears.
- Prefer descriptive anchor text over generic labels.
- Keep primary navigation focused on major content families and user tasks.
- Use breadcrumbs where hierarchy is meaningful.
- Keep orphan pages out of the published information architecture unless intentional.
- Generate related content from explicit relationships or meaningful taxonomy, not arbitrary keyword overlap.
- Do not create circular collections of thin pages solely to manufacture links.
- Keep link destinations locale consistent when an equivalent translation exists.
- Validate internal references at build time when the content model permits it.
