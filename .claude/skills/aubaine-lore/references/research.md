# Research

Outside research that shaped this skill: each finding with its source, what aubaine-lore took from it
and what it refused, and the date it was read. Aubaine takes techniques from other games and books,
never their lore, names or wording, and a page never describes Aubaine against another game. This
file names its sources by the owner's choice of 2026-11-07
(`docs/adr/0072-the-lore-run-is-one-writer-and-five-read-only-reviewers-over-a-fact-map.md`).

## How to use this file

- Read it before a run. Research again only for a question it does not answer, or when the row that
  answers it is more than twelve months old.
- `aubaine-lore-researcher` returns rows; the orchestrator adds them here, dated.
- Rows are grouped by the topic each reference owns, and a new question adds rows to its topic.
  `(secondary)` in Source marks a row that rests on a review or a summary, and `(unverified)` one the
  researcher could not check.
- A finding becomes practice only in the reference that owns the concept, stated as Aubaine's own;
  this file keeps where it came from.
- The PDFs under `tmp/dnd/` and `tmp/srd/` are local copies, read page by page and never copied into
  the repository. A page number is the PDF page, with the printed one when it differs.

through how characters react during play. Across the D&D books an epigraph, the lore, the names and
the traits mostly come in that order, though one book drops the epigraph and the history and another
moves the names from entry to entry. Four of them never ask the player a question in their people
chapters: their questions speak to the DM or sit in the backgrounds.
