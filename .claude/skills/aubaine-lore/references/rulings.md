# The owner's lore rulings

Canon the owner settled in conversation that no page carries yet, the owner's direction for each
people, and the owner's standing requests. Each entry states the ruling in this file's own words,
never the owner's message, with its date. Canon and direction ask for no sentence. A standing
request is a request meant to hold beyond the run that recorded it: it asks for what it names, and
its entry gives the file and line of the owner's words. Read the entries of every people, place and
event a run touches before writing its brief.

Once a page carries a fact, the page is the authority: the entry then keeps only what the ruling
excluded, and names the page. A ruling is reopened only by the owner. A standing request holds until
the owner withdraws it; the trim applies to canon. A value chosen for one page, such as a heading, is
not a ruling; it lives in the page.

## Elves

### Standing requests

- **2026-11-07.** The lore of the owner's Notion export of the elf page that the page had lost (the
  elvish tongue, the ancient line, the watchful grace, the long view) comes back into the chapters
  where it fits (`tmp/lore-runs/2026-11-07-elfe-revise/request.md:1690`). The page carries it.

### Direction

- **2026-11-07.** Elven culture draws on solarpunk and on the old Gaelic and Celtic cultures. It is
  direction for the writer: it chooses which existing facts come forward and which images fit. It
  never creates a fact, and published copy never names it.
- **2026-11-07.** The opening says what an elf is (the owner's own summary paragraph), never history.
  The Matriarchie is written as the natural order of elven life, never as an exception. The chapters
  take narrative titles.
- **2026-11-08.** « druidique » is no label for anything elven: the owner struck its five uses on the
  elf page as a stock word that dressed the elves as druids
  (`tmp/lore-runs/2026-11-07-elfe-revise/request.md:1768`).

### Canon

- **2026-11-03.** In « Touchée par leur supplice », the word is « supplice », as the owner wrote it.
- **2026-11-03.** Only the metals are what the elves refuse to mine; the rare goods they trade for are
  foodstuffs.
- **2026-11-03.** Elves live in villages too, but their great cities are their signature and read as
  solarpunk, the way the human regions read as medieval, Victorian or steampunk. The page describes
  that architecture from inside the world and never names the genre.
- **2026-11-07.** La Matriarchie is the owner's own word for how elven societies are organised, as
  other cultures were patriarchies. On the lore page it is capitalised and is the only term for it,
  never « matriarcat » beside it. In « Jouer un Elfe » the owner writes « Vous avez grandi sous une
  matriarchie », lower case with its article, and that wording stays.
- **2026-11-07.** Tiardrassil is the world-tree whose sanctuary Tiar prays in, one arbre-monde among
  several. The names Teyrdrassil and Tyrdrassil are retired.
- **2026-11-07.** The war of Brécelann ended with a last battle that bears the war's name. It was
  fought near Glenmor, a small village where the forest begins. After the massacre on both sides,
  Jacques « Le Scellaire » Arsenault called for peace and mediated between Arthur Colbert and Tiar,
  and the traité de Glenmor ended the war. Arthur was not yet king at that dawn
  (`data/lore/species/humain.md`).
- **2026-11-07.** Élyséa's guidance is to let life be life: every life is equal.
- **2026-11-07.** At the dawn of that last battle, Tiar is confused and angry, in a war she never
  wanted. She has believed all her life in a goddess who does not help her, finds it unfair after all
  she and her ancestors did, and asks why Élyséa lets so many of her people die. She already fights
  in the battles to protect her own, and she is torn between doing justice herself and Élyséa's
  guidance. She is close to losing her faith, and her own people are disgusted with her because she
  seems to be failing.
- **2026-11-07.** Élyséa's teaching in French reads « laisser la vie suivre son cours, car toutes
  les vies se valent »; in English, the owner's "let life be life".
- **2026-11-07.** The decisions taken on 2026-11-04 for the elf page are withdrawn, apart from the
  Matriarchie: its outline, the veillées de la Canopée having no power, the shape of « Jouer un
  Elfe », the removal of the orcs, trolls and goblins from the page, and the name Tyrdrassil. They
  carry no weight; a run decides them again from the canon.
- **2026-11-07.** The magic Élyséa taught the last Galwarians draws on sap, wind, chlorophyll and the
  whole of the surrounding nature, no longer on sap and chlorophyll against the ancient fire. Since
  then the magic of nature runs in the elven clans.
- **2026-11-07.** As the first heirs of the Galwarians that humans met, the elves are the people
  most associated with the Galwarians. It is said that they resemble them most and that every other
  lineage has strayed too far from them: both stay what is said, never the narrator's fact. The elves
  are known for reminding anyone who does not know it yet that a few millennia ago their ancestors
  ruled this earth and its magic (a few centuries until the owner's change of 2026-11-09,
  `tmp/lore-runs/2026-11-07-elfe-revise/request.md:1816`).
- **2026-11-07.** The page no longer says which peoples share Élyséa's gift or why she refused it to
  the orcs, the trolls and the goblins: that paragraph was about other peoples. The shared gift stays
  in the prerequisite of `SAVELY-001`; the refusal is on no page.
- **2026-11-07.** « La Grande Guerre » is the war of Brécelann, named so only in « Jouer un Elfe ».
  Elsewhere the war keeps the name of Brécelann.
- **2026-11-07.** « Jouer un Elfe » asks the player nothing: it states what an elf grows up with.
- **2026-11-08.** Villages and cities sometimes unite under a Reinauté, the rule of a queen, and form
  a Reinaume, the realm she rules, such as the Reinaume de Brécelann. In English the realm is a
  Queendom; the Reinauté has no English word yet. In a Reinaume the reine régente decides, helped and
  counselled by four matrons who each represent great cities or clans. Smaller autonomous villages
  and groups decide by consensus at the veillées de la Canopée. The councils of matrons no longer
  govern the great cities, and the real monarchy the owner compared it with goes on no page
  (`tmp/lore-runs/2026-11-07-elfe-revise/request.md:1772` and `:1778`).
- **2026-11-09.** The magic of nature runs in the clans descended from the last Galwarians, the
  elves' among them, and no longer in the elven clans alone. The last Galwarians also gave rise to the
  halflings, the Fir Bolg and the drows; under the 2026-11-07 ruling on Élyséa's gift the elf page
  names none of them as an heir of the last Galwarians
  (`tmp/lore-runs/2026-11-07-elfe-revise/request.md:1826`).
- **2026-11-09.** Every Reinaume has its seat of power, the Matronobriga, where the political life of
  the Reinaume is debated and decided; the name keeps its spelling in English
  (`tmp/lore-runs/2026-11-07-elfe-revise/request.md:1820`).
- **2026-11-08.** By the end of her prayer Tiar has lost enough faith in Élyséa to warn her: her last
  words give a goddess a last warning (`tmp/lore-runs/2026-11-07-elfe-revise/request.md:1767`).
