# Flavor writing

Flavor must add usable identity.

Anchor flavor in at least one of these:

- a visible behavior
- a material or texture
- a sound
- a smell
- a local custom
- a practical consequence
- a social reaction
- a ritual
- a cost
- a trace left behind

Do not use flavor as decorative fog.

Default fantasy filler is refused by the check rather than by taste. `BANNED_PHRASES` in
`tests/data/integrity.test.ts` carries the set. What this file adds is the positive test: an image
earns its place by being anchored above, and by nothing else.

Do not open every entry with atmospheric scenery.

Do not end every entry with a dramatic summary sentence.

If one sensory image is enough, stop there.

## Where flavor goes

Lore is not flavor. A species or tree lore page tells history in its own register, held in
`lore.md`.

The item `description` is the flavor field. It is one or two sentences and it sits apart from the
mechanical `text`.

A skill or state `description` is mechanical. A sentence of colour may open it when it sets up the
mechanic that follows, as `Vous entrez en rage.` does, but it must not imply anything the rules do
not state.

A tree carries a subtitle, which is the one place a single evocative line is the whole job.

Flavor never creates a mechanic. If a reader could act on a sentence at the table, it is a rule and
belongs in the rules, spelled out.
