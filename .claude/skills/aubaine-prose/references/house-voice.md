# House voice

The default voice is confident, concrete, compact, and gameable.

Write as if the reader opened the page because they intend to use the information at the table.

Prefer:

- specific nouns
- active verbs
- physical details
- direct causal language
- short paragraphs with one purpose each
- concrete consequences
- restrained imagery

Avoid:

- promotional copy
- self-conscious cleverness
- inflated fantasy diction
- vague grandeur
- generic mystery language
- mood that obscures function

A strong paragraph usually contains one memorable detail, not five ornamental ones.

Do not imitate the prose of a specific living author or commercial book line. Build an original
project voice.

## How the voice differs by register

Rule text is the tightest register in the codex. Second person, present tense, no ornament, one
clause per idea. The reader is mid turn and needs to execute.

The item `description` is the only place in an entry where the voice relaxes. One or two sentences,
concrete, physical, and never implying a mechanic. `Lame de ceinture : on la garde à portée de main,
on la lance au besoin.` is the length and the tone.

Book chapters speak to a player learning the system. They may use a full paragraph, an aside, and a
worked example, but they still lead with the definition.
