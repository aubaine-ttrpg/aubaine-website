---
title: "Species"
description: "What a Species brings to a character, and how to choose its two Skills."
---

Species is the first of the three choices in step 1, alongside Archetype and Domain.

## What a Species fixes

It brings two things at creation:

- **two Species Skills**, kept from among those your Species and, if you have one, your subspecies or regional origin offer or impose. They are Memorised without taking up any Memory and do not have to be bought. Once creation is over, you can no longer change them;
- **your Movement**, where it differs from the default 9 metres.

Both are granted outright. They cost neither XP nor MP, and they do not count against the 22 MP or the 25 XP the GM gives you.

A Species may also name the languages you speak, read and write.

Some Species take their Size and their Movement from another Species, and their page says which.

## Keeping two of the Skills offered

A Species offers more Skills than it grants. You keep two at creation. The rest are not lost: they stay buyable afterwards in the Common Bank, at the price printed on the Skill, and their Prerequisite reserves them to your Species. You memorise a Species Skill bought this way like any other, and it takes 1 Memory while it is Memorised.

Two characters of the same Species therefore do not start with the same ground covered.

A Species that offers only two Skills leaves no choice: you take both.

## Subspecies and regional origins

Some Species divide into subspecies, presented on the Species page. A few call theirs regional origins: the two names mean the same thing, and each page uses the one its Species goes by. You choose yours at creation, and you then keep your two Skills from among those of your Species and your subspecies.

A subspecies may impose one of those two Skills, and the Species page marks it as imposed. That one is yours without choosing, and you pick the second from the other Skills of your Species and your subspecies.

The Skills of a subspecies carry that subspecies as a Prerequisite. A character from another subspecies cannot buy them.

## Where to read the Species

The Species index carries the current list, and each Species page its Size, its Movement, its subspecies or regional origins, its Skills and how settled its writing is. That index is what holds, not this chapter.

A Species still in Draft carries its badge. While a table plays on entries that are not settled, the GM announces the Species available and the Skills each one offers.
