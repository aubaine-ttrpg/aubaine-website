The site's icons, fonts, sounds, textures and heraldic drawings come from other projects and keep their own licences. The icon, font and heraldry licences ask to be credited, which this page does. The sounds and textures are under CC0, which asks for nothing; they are credited here all the same.

The arms of Victoria adapt four WappenWiki charges: Lion Rampant, Estoile, Book Closed and Raven. Each is recoloured gold, given a quarter of the shield and rendered in chased gilt relief between purple and black enamels. Aubaine composed the arms in Armoria, which also provides the heater shield and its wavy partition line; like the rest of the game content, they are shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Aubaine claims no rights over the flags in the language picker.
